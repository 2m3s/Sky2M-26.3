# Changelog

All notable changes to Sky2M for Minecraft 26.3 are listed here, newest first. The 26.3 build has its own version numbers, starting again at 1.0; the 26.2 build keeps its own changelog.

## 1.0.4

### Added
- Party Finder helper (Dungeons > Party Finder): groups you can't join get a dark red slot and groups missing your class a green one (both on by default), with optional red for paid carries, purple for non-PUGs and yellow when a member's class level is at or below a set level. Each group's tooltip lists the missing classes (yours in green) and shows member lines in colour, and the floor (F7, M5, E) is written on groups and on the Catacombs Gate and Select Floor menus. SkyHanni's Party Finder settings can be imported.
- Accepted / Refused Glow (Garden > Visitors, on by default): a visitor glows green after you accept its offer and red after you refuse it, while it walks off.
- Clip sharing (Clips): the "Clip saved" message gets an [Upload] button like screenshots. It uploads the clip and gives you the link with [Send in /s2c], [Copy Link] and [Watch]. Clip links in /s2c show as [Watch], which plays the clip in game in a menu with sound (with Sky2M Clipping 1.0.1 or newer), or in your browser without it.
- Missing Accessories (/s2 mp): a screen listing the accessories you don't have yet, cheapest Magical Power first (coins per MP), with upgrades for families you own a lower tier of (priced after selling the tier you have, or by crafting), Pulse Ring rarity upgrades (Thunder in a Bottle), and Recombobulate rows for your accessories that aren't recombobulated yet, all sorted together, with the MP each adds, its price and where to get it (AH, Bazaar, craft, forge or NPC). Accessories that can't be obtained (Artifact of Space, Eternal Crystal and the rest of the item repo's ignored list) are never listed. Click a row to search for it. Soulbound, untradeable and unpriced ones are listed at the bottom. The first /s2 mp loads your accessories from your profile, then the list keeps itself up to date in the background and as soon as a new accessory reaches your inventory or bag. Sort by coins per MP, price or MP gain, hide upgrades, and search. Settings in Inventory > Items & Tooltips.
- Sack Display (Inventory > Sacks & Item Tracking, on by default): while a sack is open, a panel beside it lists each item with its icon, amount (and capacity), price and value, and the sack's total. Each sack keeps its own price source: NPC Sell, Bazaar Insta-Sell, Sell Offer, Insta-Buy or Lowest BIN. Click Price on the panel to change it for that sack, or [Default] to use the default again. Sort by value, amount or name, and empty items are hidden.
- Import settings from other mods in a screen: /s2 import [mod], the Import From Other Mods button in General settings, or the overlapping-mods chat notice. It lists every Sky2M setting the other mod also has, with both values and On / Off / Skip for each. It works with SkyHanni, Skyblocker, Devonian, NoFrills, NoammAddons, Odin, SkyOcean, SBO, Custom Scoreboard, SkyblockAddons, RFU, Stella, SkyblockExtras and NoPo (525 settings). Only SkyBlock mods are listed, and only those with at least one setting Sky2M has. For other SkyBlock mods, settings whose names match are offered as guesses that start on Skip.
- Click to Open Bazaar (Visitor Shopping List, on by default): while a menu is open, click an item in the shopping list to search the Bazaar for it; the item is highlighted green in the results.
- Pest Plot Borders (Garden > Pest Finder, on by default): a red see-through wall around every plot that has pests, visible from a distance and through blocks.
- Support the October 27 Garden update: the Visitor Timer handles Max Occupancy queues of up to 10 visitors (it learns the size when the queue is full, or set Max Occupancy 0-5) and shows "7/10 visitors"; the visitor cooldown is learned from the tab list, so Better Advertising is picked up by itself. Ghost Visitors' mutation requests are listed even when the item list doesn't know them, Drops Statistics counts Ectoplasm, Taylor shows as SPECIAL, and the four bonus Spooky Festivals (Oct 28-31) are in the event calendar.
- Item Icons for the Visitor Shopping List (on by default): each item shows as its icon with the amount (and price and sack count when those are on) instead of its name.
- Garden Drops (Combat > Rare Drops, off by default): farming dyes and the Ray of Helios in the Garden play the RNG Drop Totem animation and show on the RNG HUD. This replaces the Garden's separate Special Drop Animation (its setting carries over).
- Harvest Feast Crop Rotation (Events > Harvest Feast, on by default): a HUD in the Garden with the crops in season during the Harvest Feast (or Grand Feast), how long until they rotate, and which crops come in season next and when. This month's rotation is fetched from eliteskyblock.com (Fetch Feast Data, on), and opening Feast Chef Ted's All Crops menu updates it. Show Outside Garden shows it everywhere. Move it in /s2 gui.
- Show Session XP (Skill Progress > Display, off by default): the Skill Progress display shows how much XP you've gained in the shown skill this session ("Session: +12,345"), since you started the game or switched profile.
- Chat Emotes (Chat > Chat Tweaks, on by default): Hypixel's MVP++ chat emotes for everyone. o/, <3, :shrug:, :tableflip:, :yes:, :star:, :dab: and the rest turn into the emote when you send them in chat or with /pc, /gc, /ac, /msg and the other chat commands, so everyone sees it. A message that would get too long is sent as typed.
- Longer Chat History (Chat > Chat Tweaks, on by default): the chat never deletes old lines (Unlimited Chat History, on by default), so you can scroll back to the start of the session instead of Minecraft's last 100. With Unlimited off, Chat History Lines sets how many it keeps (100 to 10000).
- Garden Custom Keybinds (Garden > Custom Keybinds, off by default): while you hold a farming tool in the Garden with no menu open, attack, use, the movement keys, jump and sneak follow the keys you set here. Keys left as NONE work as normal.
- Add-ons (bottom of /s2): shows the features of an approved Sky2M add-on when one is installed. Without one the category isn't there.
- Resource pack fonts (Misc > Fonts): font resource packs in config/sky2m/fonts or your resourcepacks folder show in the Font dropdown next to Default and Inter, and draw all text without turning the pack on. Packs that only replace the font texture (font overlays) work too; spaces and Hypixel's symbols keep Minecraft's look.
- Guess Hotspot Radar (Fishing > Extras, on by default): right-click a Hotspot Radar and the flame trail it shows is followed to the hotspot, marked with a HOTSPOT label, its distance and (Line to Hotspot, on) a line from your crosshair; it clears when you get within 10 blocks.
- Value Breakdown Panel (Inventory > Economy, on by default): hovering an item in a menu or your inventory shows a coloured panel (item icon, labels coloured by type, green + gold prices) beside it listing what its Estimated Item Value is made of (base item, stars, recombobulator, enchantments, gemstones, scrolls and other upgrades) with the total: single upgrades first, then Rod Parts, Enchantments (the five most expensive, the rest summed) and Gemstones under their own headings. Fishing rod parts (hook, line, sinker) now count towards the value. While it is on, the Estimated Item Value line is no longer in the Price Tooltip.
- Command Tab Complete (Chat > Command Autofill): Tab suggests player names and subcommands for /p, /f, /g, /msg, /w, /visit, /pv, /invite, /ah, /boop and /trade (players on your island, your party, friends from /f list and, if turned on, guild members from /g online), /warp destinations, your sack items for /gfs, the slots for /show and the VIP islands for /visit.
- Custom Wardrobe (Inventory, on by default): the Wardrobe as cards showing you in each armour set, with page tabs, favourite stars, an Only Favourites button and hover tooltips for each piece; click a card to equip it. Pick a theme (Sky2M, Midnight, Forest, Sunset, Ocean, Crimson, Candy, Light, Glass) or make your own with Custom's colours; the Theme button in the wardrobe cycles them.
- Edit HUDs Key (GUI > HUDs): a keybind that opens /s2 gui, also while a menu is open, so HUDs that only show in a menu (like the Chocolate Factory Stats) can be moved with the menu behind them.
- Chocolate Factory Stats (Events > Chocolate Factory, on by default): while the Chocolate Factory is open, a HUD with stats in Sky2M's own compact layout: your chocolate, all-time and this prestige, time and chocolate to prestige, production per second, minute, hour and day, multiplier, barn, Time Tower charges and when they are full, leaderboard position and time until the best upgrade.
- Skill Level As Stack Size (Skill Progress, on by default): in the Your Skills menu each skill's level shows on its icon like a stack size (Combat 58 shows 58), and past the max the overflow level (70) with Overflow > Skill Menu Stack Size.
- Items on Ground (Slayers > Loot & Profit, on by default): while you have a slayer quest in that slayer's area, items on the ground within 15 blocks show their name in its rarity colour, amount and price, drawn through blocks; ones worth less than the Minimum Price (50k) aren't labelled.
- Fonts (Misc > Fonts): draw all text in Inter or any .ttf you put in config/sky2m/fonts, picked from the Font dropdown (Default goes back to Minecraft's font). Inter is built in and drawn sharp at any size. Size and Height adjust it; Sharpness is for .ttf fonts. Only letters, digits and punctuation change: symbols like Hypixel's stat icons (❤ ❈ ✦ ☠ ⚔) keep their look.
- Cocoon Label (Combat > Cocoon Alert, on by default): every mob you cocoon is named over its cocoon with the time until it hatches (once Sky2M has seen one hatch), even when the alert title is off or only for important mobs.
- Highlight Auctions (Inventory, on by default): in Manage Auctions your sold auctions are green and expired ones red; "Highlight Underbid BINs" (off) also marks BINs priced above the item's value.
- Pet Rarity (Chat > Chat Tweaks, on by default): pet drop lines say the pet's rarity in its colour, "PET DROP! Epic Slug" in purple or "Legendary Slug" in gold. Also for pets in RARE DROP! and fishing catch lines and Sky2M's +Drop message; hover and click parts of the message are kept.
- Profit Trackers: pick each item's price — right-click an item to cycle NPC Sell, Insta-Sell, Sell Order, Auction or Custom (only sources with a price), or left-click it to set the source and a custom value next to its amount. Totals and profit per hour use it, in every tracker and /s2 session.
- Compact Bestiary (Chat > Chat Tweaks, on by default): Hypixel's BESTIARY level-up block becomes one line; hover it for the rewards, click it to open the Bestiary. Milestones get their own line.
- SkyBlock XP in Chat (Chat > Chat Tweaks, on by default): the "+5 SkyBlock XP (Accessories) (35/100)" from the action bar is also sent to chat.
- "Sky Mall & Lottery: Everywhere" (Chat > Spam Filters): hide or move the Sky Mall and Lottery buff messages on the mining and foraging islands too.

### Changed
- Inventory HUD: Rarity Background (on by default) puts each SkyBlock item on its rarity colour, in the shape and opacity set under Item Rarity, even with Item Rarity off.
- Profile viewer Overflow Levels now covers pets: a maxed pet keeps levelling past 100 (or 200), each level taking the XP of its last one, shown in purple on the Pets page and the main page's active pet, with progress to the next level in the tooltip.
- Custom Wardrobe: a Toggle button under the Wardrobe (below Close and Favourites in the custom one, below the chest in the regular one; green when the custom wardrobe is on, red when off) switches the custom wardrobe on ("Toggle Custom Wardrobe" in the regular Wardrobe) or off ("Toggle Off Custom Wardrobe" in the custom one).
- `/s2 featuresearch [text]` opens the feature search (same as `/s2 find`), and you can describe what a feature does instead of knowing its name: filler words are skipped, word endings don't matter ("pests" finds "pest"), and features matching most of the words are listed after exact matches.
- Garden plot borders (Sprayonator and the plot border key) are a see-through yellow wall 3 blocks tall at your height instead of a tall blue box with lines across the sky.
- Sack menus no longer show the Estimated Item Value: not in the Value Breakdown Panel, the Shift breakdown or the Price Tooltip.
- The old chat-only SkyHanni / Skyblocker import (3 settings) is replaced by the import screen.
- Visitor Timer: "Sixth Visitor Estimate" is now "Next Visitor When Full" and "Sixth Visitor Warning" is "Next Visitor Ready Warning", for any queue size ("Queue Full, next in 2m 3s"). The count no longer adds a phantom extra visitor, the warning title shows once per estimate, and New Visitor Ping no longer pings while the queue is full. Your old choices carry over.
- Rename "SkyHanni Farming" to "Farming Features".
- Move the Safari Ticket Reminder from Garden to Foraging (your setting carries over).
- Remove other mods' and people's names from setting names, descriptions and dropdowns. Some options were renamed: Terminal Solver styles Overlay and Centred Panel, Alternate Device Solvers, Map Styles Classic, Detailed and Minimal, Safari Checklist style Compact, Rare SC party message style Surfaced, Croesus Alternate Colours, and the Cross-Mod Bridge's Melody, Dungeon Data and Secrets Sockets.
- The same item dropping twice within 3 seconds plays the RNG Drop Totem animation once.
- Game tests are kept local and no longer pushed to GitHub; the build skips them when the folder is missing.
- Tidy the source layout: the loose classes, mixins and miscellaneous features are sorted into clearer folders. Nothing changes in game, and the /s2 menu looks the same.
- Livid (F5/M5) uses the same Livid Finder as 26.2: it boxes and labels the real Livid with a line to her, can hide the wrong ones (Hide Wrong Livids) and has a Color Override, instead of the old Livid Solver.
- Command Tab Complete learns your friends from Hypixel's "Friend > Name joined/left" messages too, so /pv and the other commands suggest them without running /f list first.
- Sky2M's screens fit better on GUI scale Auto: while one is open, the GUI is one step smaller (1080p: 3 instead of 4), so text and buttons aren't cut off. Other GUI scales, the HUD editor and menus Sky2M draws over are unchanged.
- Buttons in Sky2M's own screens look like the settings' buttons, so every screen matches /s2.
- Fishing Profit only shows while you're fishing (a rod in your hand, your bobber out, or a catch in the last 3 minutes), like the Sea Creature Tracker, instead of following you to Kuudra, the End or the Rift.
- The slayer tracker, RNG HUD and loadout lines are only written to the log while /s2 debug is on.
- Settings missing from a saved config now get the same default a new install gets.
- /s2 calc also takes x (or ×) for multiply, like 5x3 or 2.5m x 4.
- /pv's Armour & Equipment page shows only its slots: four armour pieces on top and four equipment pieces below, instead of two rows of nine.
- Right-clicking an item in the Item List or on a recipe shows the recipes it is used in (Item List > Right Click is now Show Usages by default, also for settings on Wiki or Recipes).
- The Farming RNG HUD is now the RNG HUD, in Misc instead of Farming (your settings move with it), with each line centred.
- RNG HUD names drops in the item's own rarity colour from the item list first; the chat message's colour is only a fallback.
- Cocoon Alert is on by default. Its title is still only for slayer bosses, elusive mobs and important bosses; turn off "Only Important Mobs" to get it for every mob you cocoon (like "CAUGHT! You cocooned a Alligator!").
- Profile Icons In Chat (Ironman ♲, Bingo Ⓑ, Stranded ☀) is now on by default, also for settings saved before.
- Item Pickup Log works: item names in their rarity colour (sacks, shards, pets and enchanted books too), the coin value line on top, what you buy or move while a menu is open shows once it closes, nothing is logged for 2 seconds after a world change, each item's + and - lines expire together, and Alignment is now Top, Center or Bottom (which way the log grows).
- Spam Filters' Empty Lines also catches lines of only spaces or colour codes.
- GitHub releases and their download are named "Sky2M Mod" instead of "SkyBalls Mod".

### Fixed
- Fix dungeon map player heads stacking on top of another teammate or not showing, in all three map styles: a teammate out of render distance could be given a nearby teammate's map marker. Nearby teammates now claim their own marker first.
- Fix the Minimal dungeon map drawing player heads away from the room they're in (further off towards the map's edges).
- Fix protected items (/s2 protect) being blocked in the Farming and Hunting Toolkits and the Abiphone, so protected tools couldn't be swapped.
- Fix Keep Terrain Loaded never turning on when the scoreboard is too full to show the server line (the Garden during Jacob's Contest): it reads the server from the tab list instead.
- Fix Shard Level Up Highlight and the shard tracker missing Hypixel's new "Syphon 2 more to level up!" lore, so shards you had enough of weren't highlighted.
- Fix the Skill ETA display showing a maxed skill as 60➜61 (Farming at overflow level 73) while timing the overflow level: it shows the overflow level it counts towards (73➜74), also with ETA overflow off.
- Fix Mouse Lock letting the camera turn for a split second after closing a menu while holding a farming tool.
- Fix /s2 mp showing new accessories the item list only has in colours (the 7th Anniversary Cake Hat) as a barrier named CAKE_HAT_2026: they get the item's icon and name.
- Fix Farming and Mining profit trackers counting compacted items as the raw item: what a compactor turns your drops into (Enchanted or Compacted Moonflower, Mutant Nether Wart, Box of Seeds and the rest) is now shown instead, including compactions straight into your sacks, without counting anything twice.
- Fix the Garden drop animation playing for other players' dye drops and for player chat.
- Fix visitors that should keep their chat (like Spaceman) being hidden by Hide Chat, and Item Preview not showing "Unknown" rewards: the visitor data's flags weren't read.
- Fix enchant colouring skipping a tool's enchant list: Minecraft's own enchant line under the name (like "Efficiency V" on farming tools) got coloured instead. It's now hidden (Hide Vanilla Enchants under Enchant Parsing, on by default), so only Hypixel's enchant list shows and gets coloured.
- Fix the Sea Creature Tracker showing over /storage: it's hidden in the storage overview, ender chest and backpack pages (and Sky2M's storage screens), and still shows over other menus.
- Fix Command Tab Complete never learning your friends: Hypixel sends each /f list page as one message, which wasn't read. Opening /f list now fills /pv, /p, /msg and the rest with your friends (and /g online or /g list with your guild).
- Fix Estimated Item Value counting master stars on fishing rods and Kuudra armour: Kuudra armour is now priced by its ten essence stars (Crimson Essence and Heavy Pearls), shown star by star in the breakdown, and items with no stars of their own, like fishing rods, get no star cost. Master stars stay for dungeon items.
- Dungeon splits don't time a part whose start wasn't seen, so a missed start can't print a time like 1793018752s or save a 0.20s PB over a real one. Reconnecting keeps the run's splits, and joining a run already going picks the timer up from the sidebar's Time Elapsed (only parts seen from their start count for PBs).
- Skill Progress > Progress Bar > Textured Bar Width and Height work.
- HUDs don't draw over the Held Item editor (/s2 helditem).
- Setting descriptions that said "Move it in /s2 hud" now say /s2 gui, and Use /pv no longer says it's off by default.
- Slayer profit takes the tier from the sidebar's quest line when a boss's name tag doesn't show it, so its quest cost is never counted as 0.
- Slayer profit always takes the quest's cost out, as the wiki's price for the slayer and tier (100k for a T5 Tarantula, whatever discount you got; Riftstalker costs motes, so 0), instead of "unknown" or a misread purse change.
- Cocoon Label counts down to the hatch from the first cocoon (about 6.5s until one has been timed), and says "Hatching..." if it runs over.
- The boss HUD finds your slayer boss again after it hatches from a cocoon (its "Spawned by" tag stays with the cocoon).
- Cocoon Label shows over cocoons that appear together with (or just before) the "CAUGHT!" / "YOU COCOONED YOUR SLAYER BOSS" message.
- Protected items (/s2 protect) can be put into The Hex, like the anvil and enchanting table.
- Slayer profit: with auto-slayer, the spawn fee taken from your bank counts for the quest it starts, not the one that just ended, so the profit line no longer says "Slayer cost: unknown" (for every slayer). A purse change of a few coins at the quest start is no longer taken for the fee.
- Slayer Boss Phase HUD: your boss no longer goes missing (with only minibosses showing) when someone else's boss is next to it, when the player picked with the boss-cycle key has no boss nearby, when "Spawned by" shows your name with a rank, or when Hypixel puts an icon before "SLAYER BOSS" and leaves out the "Spawned by" line.
- Fix the Item Pickup Log adding different pets (and enchanted books) into one line and valuing them at the wrong price.

## 1.0.3

### Added
- Item List recipes: hold Shift over an ingredient needed more than once (like 32 Wheat) to see its prices for the whole amount, with the price each.
- Enter Confirms Signs (Inventory, on by default): press Enter in a SkyBlock sign (an auction price like 1m, a bazaar amount) to send it, like clicking Done.
- Item List sorting: a sort button beside the search bar (and Sort By / Reverse Sort settings) orders items by Price (most expensive first), Rarity, Type or Name; right click reverses it. Search `type:armor`, `type:sword` or `rarity:legendary` to narrow the list, and `armor` now finds every helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots.
- Live chat calculator: typing `/s2 calc 8*9`, or just `8*9`, shows "= 72" at the end of the chat box as you type, without pressing Enter.
- [Upload to Website] after uploading a screenshot (and an "Upload to Website" button in `/s2 screenshots`): puts it on shadowisabot.com/screenshots with your name and head.
- Armor Stack Display (Combat > HUDs & Counters): your Crimson, Terror, Aurora or Fervor armor stacks in a movable HUD, with the time until the next stack decays.
- `-Dsky2m.hypixelHosts=<address,...>` launch option for testing on a local Hypixel-style server: the listed addresses count as Hypixel for Discord Rich Presence, the self-test and other address checks. It does nothing unless set, and the cross-mod bridge still only connects on the real hypixel.net.
- Coins Per Hour HUD (Misc): graphs your purse per minute, with optional last slayer boss profit and powder goal ETA lines.
- Skill achievements for Farming, Mining, Combat, Foraging, Fishing, Enchanting, Alchemy, Carpentry, Runecrafting, Social and Taming, with named tiers and symbols, plus a Skills tab in `/s2 achievements`.
- Settings profiles: `/s2 profile save|load|list|delete <name>` keeps named copies of your settings.
- One-time warning when other installed mods overlap Sky2M features, with a setting to turn it off.
- `/s2 import` carries over a few matching settings from other installed mods.
- `/s2 health`: Sky2M version, startup time, Sky2M chat connection, related mods and this session's error count.
- HUD Theme setting (GUI) with High contrast, Deuteranopia, Protanopia and Tritanopia palettes for text HUDs.
- RNG Meter Advisor HUD and `/s2 rngadvice`: the slayer RNG meter closest to dropping.
- Trophy Fish tracker (Fishing): counts each trophy fish by tier, with a HUD and `/s2 trophyfish`.
- Mineshaft Stats HUD (Mining): time in the shaft and blocks broken, with an optional summary when you leave.
- Mentions (Chat): highlight your name and keywords in chat with a colour and sound.
- Recipe Cost line in price tooltips (Inventory > Price Tooltips): the cost to craft the item from its recipe, with how much it saves or costs versus buying, Shift for the ingredient list, and an option to include cheaper sub-crafts.
- Remove All Fog (Misc > Visual > Random): removes every kind of fog, including the fog in water, lava, powder snow, darkness and blindness, on your own screen.
- Dungeons > Run Recap: a Death Recap (who died, to what, in which room and when; `/s2 deathrecap`, also saved in Run History), a Class Cooldowns HUD for Bonzo's Mask, Spirit Mask, Phoenix Pet and your class ultimate, a Run Summary card (`/s2 runsummary` opens it; with "Post Run Summary to /s2c" on, one /s2c message with a clickable [View] opens the full summary for anyone who clicks it; nothing goes to party or guild chat) and editable Boss Phase Notes per floor.
- Route Replay (Dungeon > Run History): your path through each run is recorded and can be replayed on the dungeon map with your head, a trail through the rooms, play/pause, 1x-8x speed and a scrub bar. Use the Replay button in a run's details in `/s2 runs` or `/s2 replay` (also works for Dungeon Sim runs). New "Record Route" option.
- `/s2 rng` is now a screen with tabs (All, Combat, Slayers, Fishing, Mining, Farming, Foraging, Pets, Other) and collapsible groups: enter Magic Find, Pet Luck, Overbloom, Looting and Luck, search every drop those stats affect (M, P and O badges and filters) and see the real chance and average tries per drop (one compact row per item; the formula is in the tooltip). Dungeon and gift drops are left out, pets show their rarity colour without the level, and the End dragon pets are one "Dragon Pets" dropdown. Farming shows only the rare pest drops and Harvest Feast drops.
- Winter and Jerry tools (Events > Winter): Highlight Jerries and Line to Jerries, a Unique Gift Counter, Unique Gifting Opportunities, a Gift Profit Tracker (`/s2 resetgifttracker`), Gift Clean Display, a Frozen Treasure highlighter and a New Year Cake reminder.
- Chat rooms can have passwords or be invite-only: `/s2c create <room> [password]`, `/s2c join <room> [password]`, `/s2c room <room> invite|uninvite|kick|password|invite-only|public|private`, `/s2c rooms public`, and a clickable [Join] message when you are invited.
- Global search (`/s2 find`, `/s2 search <text>`, or an unbound key) that finds settings, HUDs and commands, shows their values and lets you toggle switches inline.
- `/s2 link` links your Minecraft account to shadowisabot.com (new Link page). With "Share Sessions to Website" on (off by default) your tracker sessions can be public on your player page; `/s2 link delete` removes everything stored.
- `/s2 staff` has new Audit, Player, Online and Announce tabs and a Features tab that lists every remote feature switch; `/s2 staff audit [n]` prints the staff audit log. New remote switches cover Sky2M chat, chat rooms, nicknames, who, emblems, leaderboards, slayer PBs, party finder stats, museum tooltips and screenshot sharing.
- Party Message Style (Fishing > Rare Sea Creatures): choose Sky2M Classic, Compact or a custom template. The Compact style sends and understands "WOAH! An Alligator just surfaced! Catch #245 after 18d 17h!" party messages, and other players' messages in that style now trigger your sea creature alerts.
- Dungeon Map styles (Dungeon > Dungeon Map > Map Style): choose between three map styles. The first has its room and door colours, checkmarks, room names and secrets, player markers and Secrets / Score / Deaths / Crypts lines; the second has its room colours, doors, room names with secret counts, ticks and player markers. All three draw from one shared model of the floor, so they work on Hypixel and in the Dungeon Sim.
- The Dungeon Map, its score and the secret waypoints and secret routes now work inside the Dungeon Sim, read from the sim's own floor.
- Kuudra suite (Crimson Isle > Kuudra): supply pick-up and drop-off waypoints with spot labels and supply times, pre-spot and missing-crate alerts with crate priority, unfinished-pile waypoints, a build helper HUD with a stun alert, a Fresh Tools timer, phase splits (HUD and chat) with personal bests per tier, chest profit with key cost in the Free and Paid chest menus, Kuudra's hitbox and health, and a Perk Menu cleaner that hides unused perks.
- Dungeon Extras (Dungeon > Dungeon Extras): secret waypoint type toggles, door room labels, a Spring Boots jump-height HUD, a dungeon creation cooldown timer (can block `/joininstance`), Hide Dungeon Clutter (healer orbs, blessings, Superboom TNT, Revive Stones, skulls, Journal Entries), Clean End, a solo class message hider, a Milestones Display, Quick Close (close dungeon chests with a movement key) and a Shard Score Message.
