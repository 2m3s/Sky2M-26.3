# Sky2M

An all-in-one Fabric mod for Hypixel SkyBlock on Minecraft 26.3. Formerly SkyBalls: if you're updating, remove the old SkyBalls jar. Your settings, keybinds and data are copied over the first time Sky2M starts.

- `/s2` (or `/s2m`) opens the settings; `/s2 gui` moves and resizes every HUD (right-click a HUD for its settings).
- `/s2 whatsnew` lists what's new in this version; `/s2 log` shows the full changelog ([CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md)).
- New features start off. Turn them on in `/s2`.
- Updates are announced in chat from [2m3s/Sky2M-26.3](https://github.com/2m3s/Sky2M-26.3/releases).

## Features

- **General:** storage search and overview, notes, calculator, craft helper and `/s2 recipe`, item customisation, command keys, rarity backgrounds, SkyBlock calendar, nicknames, SkyBlock Only mode and Auto Join SkyBlock.
- **Chat:** Sky2M global chat (`/s2c`), chat compacting, stash messages and click-to-translate (shown only to you).
- **Dungeons:** map, puzzle solvers, secret waypoints and routes, F7/M7 terminals, devices and dragons, timers, score, Livid and terracotta timers, blessings, Auto Requeue, and Party Finder Stats with a kick button (`/s2 cata <name>`).
- **Slayers and combat:** slayer HUD with boss and miniboss labels, boss phases, trackers, bestiary overlay, deployable timers and rare drops.
- **Skills:** farming and Garden visitors, mining commissions and Crystal Hollows, fishing (hotspots, bobber timer), foraging, alchemy, enchanting and hunting.
- **Items and inventory:** price tooltips, enchant parsing, Pack Disabler, Revert Master Stars, slot locking and binds, Inventory HUD, Item Pickup Log, and Bazaar & AH search with suggestions.
- **Visual:** custom crosshair (Valorant and CS2 codes), trail, GIF player and DVD screensaver.
- **Party:** `!warp`, `!allinvite`, `!pt`, `!f7` and more, `!fps`, `!ping` and `!tps`.
- **Online:** Sky2M party finder, friends, cosmetics, reports and Discord Rich Presence.

## Commands

`/s2` · `gui` · `whatsnew` · `log` · `search` · `storage` · `notes` · `recipe <item> [amount]` · `calc <sum>` · `custom` · `keys` · `nick [name]` · `chat [message]` · `pf` · `friends` · `cata <player>` · `requeue cancel` · `translate <language> <message>` · `route start|stop|next|back|clear|list|cancel` · `export` · `debug`

`/s2c [message]` sends to Sky2M chat. `/s2m` works the same as `/s2`.
